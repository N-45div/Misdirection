# Misdirection: Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 16 November 2026*

Misdirection is a single-player mixed-reality game for Meta Quest. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no online features. **It does not collect, send or sell any personal data.** Everything it uses stays on your headset.

## What the game uses, and why

| Data | Why | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| **Hand tracking** | Your hands are the controller: pinch, palm and reveal the coin. | Read live on the headset. Never stored or sent. |
| **Head direction and eye gaze** (eye gaze only on headsets that have it, and only with your permission) | The crowd's eyes follow where you look. | Read live on the headset. Never stored or sent. |
| **Room scan (scene data) and spatial anchors** | To find your table, place the crowd on open floor, and remember the table next time. | Stays on the headset. The game saves only an anchor ID on the device. |
| **Microphone** (optional, off by default) | Only if you turn on the "Look!" or clap accessibility option, to hear a short loud sound in place of the flourish. | Processed live as a loudness level on the headset. Never recorded, stored or sent. |
| **Game progress and settings** | Your trick book, stars, streak, tutorial and accessibility settings. | Saved in the game's own storage on the headset. |

Some builds also write simple gameplay logs (times of pickups and reveals, with no personal information) to the game's storage on the headset for tuning. They never leave the device.

## Sharing

The game has no network code and shares nothing with the developer or any third party. Meta's own platform (the store, system hand and eye tracking, the room scan) is covered by [Meta's privacy policy](https://www.meta.com/legal/privacy-policy/).

## Deleting your data

Uninstall the game to delete everything it saved. You can clear your room scan in the headset's **Settings → Physical space**.

## Children

The game doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
